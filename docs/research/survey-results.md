# Potential-User Survey Results

## 1. Overview

An anonymous survey was conducted with potential users of online thematic communities.

A total of 12 responses were collected.

The survey investigated:

- frequency of online-community usage;
- platforms currently used;
- common community activities;
- satisfaction with existing platforms;
- information retrieval;
- commonly experienced problems;
- usage of multiple platforms;
- importance of persistent discussions;
- importance of quick communication;
- preferred community-platform features.

This document records the collected survey evidence. Detailed interpretation and mapping of findings to potential system requirements are performed separately during the research-analysis stage.

## 8. Reasons for Multiple Platform Usage

Respondents selected several reasons for using multiple platforms, including:

- one platform being better for organized discussions;
- one platform being better for quick conversations;
- different members using different platforms;
- particular content being available only on certain platforms;
- different platforms providing different moderation or management capabilities.

## 11. Open Feedback

The optional open-ended question received limited responses.

Responses included requests related to:

- moderation;
- greater freedom;
- organization and management of online communities.

One response did not identify a specific improvement.

Because the open-ended response rate was limited, these responses are retained as individual evidence rather than treated as recurring findings.

## 12. Evidence Notes

The survey data records both positive and negative experiences.

Most respondents reported relatively high overall satisfaction with the platforms they currently use, while specific problems related to information persistence, organization, notifications, moderation, and platform fragmentation were also reported.

Both organized persistent discussions and quick informal communication received high importance ratings.

These observations are treated as collected evidence. Their significance for the proposed system is analyzed separately in the subsequent research-analysis stage.

## 13. Limitations

The survey contains 12 responses and uses convenience sampling.

The results therefore cannot be generalized to all users of online communities.

The sample is concentrated around users of Discord, Telegram, and Reddit.

The survey measures self-reported experience rather than directly observed behavior.
