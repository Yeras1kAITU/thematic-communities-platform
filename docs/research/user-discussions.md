# Potential-User Discussions

## 1. Purpose

Four short semi-structured discussions were conducted with representatives of the target user group.

The discussions supplement the structured survey with qualitative evidence about how users experience online thematic communities and how they use different communication platforms.

Participants are anonymized as P1, P2, P3, and P4.

The responses below preserve the substance of the participants' answers. Detailed cross-participant analysis and interpretation are performed separately during the research-analysis stage.

All participants gave their consent for their responses to be used for the research.

## 2. Core Questions

The following four core questions were used:

1. What are the main problems you experience when using online communities?
2. When you need a discussion or answer that can be found and reread later, what do you normally use? What do you use for quick communication?
3. Do you use multiple platforms for the same interest or community? If yes, why?
4. If you could improve one thing about the online communities you use, what would it be?

## 3. Participant P1

**Relevant community experience:** Anime-related communities, fan-art communities, and cosplay-related communities.

### Q1. Main Problems

The participant described community communication as chaotic. Useful tutorials and recommendation lists can become buried under large numbers of new messages, particularly in Discord.

The participant also mentioned unmarked spoilers and difficulty joining conversations in already established groups.

### Q2. Persistent and Quick Communication

For quick communication, the participant uses Discord and Telegram.

For more substantial questions, such as equipment recommendations or learning cosplay-related skills, the participant prefers Reddit or traditional forums because previous answers remain accessible.

The participant avoids asking substantial questions in Discord because the answers are difficult to locate later.

### Q3. Multiple Platforms

The participant uses several platforms for different purposes.

Telegram is used for news and quick updates, Discord for voice communication and shared activities, visual social platforms for artwork, and Reddit for discussions.

The participant stated that no single platform performs all of these functions equally well.

### Q4. Desired Improvement

The participant would like useful information and discussions to remain accessible instead of disappearing in a continuous chat history.

The participant suggested making useful discussions persistent and searchable without requiring manual intervention from a moderator.

## 4. Participant P2

**Relevant community experience:** Technology, programming, open-source, and gaming communities.

### Q1. Main Problems

The participant identified noise and repeated questions as significant problems.

Useful discussions can become difficult to locate among repeated beginner questions and unrelated communication.

The participant also stated that community quality depends strongly on moderation and that excessive notifications frequently result in communities being muted.

### Q2. Persistent and Quick Communication

For questions requiring persistent answers, the participant uses platforms such as Stack Overflow, GitHub discussions or issues, and Reddit.

The participant considers searchability and the ability to return to previous answers important for substantial technical questions.

Telegram and Discord are used for quick communication and short questions.

When a useful solution is discovered through chat, the participant sometimes records it in a more persistent location.

### Q3. Multiple Platforms

The participant uses different platforms for different purposes.

GitHub is used for project work and documentation, Discord for fast communication, and Telegram for announcements and local developer communities.

The participant noted that related discussions can occur across multiple platforms without being connected.

### Q4. Desired Improvement

The participant would improve search and topic organization.

The participant expressed interest in a system where previously solved questions could be identified more easily and where fast communication could coexist with persistent organized information.

## 5. Participant P3

**Relevant community experience:** Book clubs, board-game communities, and language-learning communities.

### Q1. Main Problems

The participant described communities as fragmented and sometimes difficult to follow.

It may be unclear where announcements, discussions, and other information are located.

The participant also mentioned difficulty determining whether a community remains active and hesitation when joining conversations in large established groups.

### Q2. Persistent and Quick Communication

Telegram is used for quick communication because it is already widely used by the participant's social groups.

For more substantial questions, the participant searches older Reddit threads and language-learning forums.

The participant also sometimes prefers asking friends directly because their answers are considered more trustworthy.

### Q3. Multiple Platforms

The participant uses several tools for the same interests.

A book club uses Telegram for communication, Instagram for events, and a shared document for maintaining a reading list.

For language learning, the participant uses both Discord and Reddit.

Switching between these systems was described as inconvenient but currently necessary.

### Q4. Desired Improvement

The participant would prefer a clearer environment connecting discussions, events, and useful resources.

The participant also suggested better onboarding for new members, such as introductory information or dedicated spaces for newcomers.

## 6. Participant P4

**Relevant community experience:** Gaming, programming, university gaming communities, and anime-related communities.

### Q1. Main Problems

The participant stated that different online-community platforms are optimized for different purposes.

Telegram was described as inconvenient for locating important older messages, while Discord was described as potentially confusing because of the number of channels.

### Q2. Persistent and Quick Communication

The participant uses Reddit for questions and information that should remain accessible.

Discord and Telegram are preferred for quick communication and social interaction.

### Q3. Multiple Platforms

For gaming interests, the participant uses Discord for social interaction and finding teammates, Reddit for guides and news, and Telegram for communication within a university gaming community.

Telegram channels are also used for anime-related news.

### Q4. Desired Improvement

The participant would simplify the Discord interface and improve topic organization in Telegram.

The participant also expressed interest in having regular topic-based chat functionality alongside Reddit-style persistent content.

## 7. Evidence Handling

The participant responses are treated as qualitative evidence.

The following principles are applied:

- participant identities are anonymized;
- contradictory and negative responses are retained;
- participant statements are not automatically treated as system requirements;
- reported experiences are distinguished from project interpretations;
- cross-participant patterns are analyzed separately;
- responses are not modified to make them support the proposed solution.

## 8. Research Limitations

The discussions involved four participants selected through convenience sampling.

The sample is small and is not intended to statistically represent all users of online communities.

Participants have experience with different types of communities, which provides variation in the qualitative evidence but does not eliminate sampling bias.

The discussions capture self-reported experiences rather than directly observed user behavior.
